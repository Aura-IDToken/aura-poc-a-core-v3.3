# Aura Protocol – Canonical Glossary (v3.3)

This glossary defines the legally and technically correct meaning of terms
used in the Aura Protocol repository. Deviations from these definitions
constitute a documentation error.

---

## Aura Protocol
A frozen, deterministic measurement protocol used to verify whether an AI
system acted in accordance with its declared specification.

Aura Protocol is not:
- an AI system
- a decision-making system
- a prediction model
- a software product
- a monitoring platform

Aura Protocol is a measurement instrument.

---

## PoCA (Proof of Consistent Agency)
A measurement method implemented by Aura Protocol that quantifies the
consistency between declared agent intent and observed execution.

PoCA is a scientific method, not a policy or decision framework.

---

## ARI (Agent Reliability Index)
A numerical measurement output produced by Aura Protocol representing
the degree of consistency between declared specification and execution.

ARI is a measurement value, not a compliance decision.

---

## Iron Core (v3.3)
A frozen, immutable implementation of Aura Protocol used as a regulatory
measurement instrument. Any modification creates a new instrument lineage.

---

## Measurement
A deterministic computation producing reproducible numerical output
from identical inputs across all supported architectures.

---

## Interpretation
Any policy, legal, or business logic applied to measurement outputs.
Interpretation is explicitly outside of Aura Protocol scope.

---

## Layer 0 (Measurement Layer)
The deterministic core that performs measurement only.
Layer 0 must never contain decision logic, thresholds, or policy.

---

## Layer 1 (Cryptographic Proof Layer)
The audit and verification layer that provides cryptographic non-repudiation
through Merkle trees, hash verification, and zero-knowledge proofs.

---

## Layer 2 (Policy Layer)
An external system that consumes measurement outputs and applies
context-dependent interpretation or decisions.

---

## Custodian
A human or institutional role responsible for preserving the integrity,
immutability, and availability of the frozen protocol.

---

## Instrument Lineage
A succession of measurement instruments, not software versions.
Each lineage is legally and cryptographically independent.

The cryptographic identity of each frozen protocol version is defined by
hashes and archival artifacts (e.g., M-DISC).

If these distinctions cannot be maintained, request clarification before generating text.
