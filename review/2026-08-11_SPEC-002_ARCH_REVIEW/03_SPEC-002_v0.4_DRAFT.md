## 0. Nature of this document — read before anything else

**Normative effect: NONE.** This is a *proposed delta* against SPEC-002 v0.3-DRAFT,
prepared as review output. It is not a submission, it has not entered REVIEW status, and it
does not carry the Protocol Custodian's authority.

**It is a DELTA, not a replacement.** It must be read alongside v0.3-DRAFT. This form was
chosen deliberately: v0.3 is the product of three recorded revision cycles (its Appendices B
and C), and silently reissuing 631 lines of merged history as "v0.4" would make it impossible
for a reviewer to see what actually changed. Task rule 20 — *"Nie przepisuj wcześniejszych
decyzji po cichu"* — is better served by an explicit delta. Sections of v0.3 not named here
are **unchanged and remain in force as drafted**.

**It creates no architectural decisions.** Every addition below is a *contract-surface*
requirement of the form "the future specification MUST define X". None of them chooses X.
This is the same discipline v0.3 applies to itself (§1, §3.4), and it is what GOV-001 §9 and
Constitution Article VIII permit an AI author to produce.

**Why a v0.4 is proposed at all.** v0.3 is a well-built document, and the review found its
requirements sound. But the review surfaced **contract surface that v0.3 does not cover** —
areas where two conformant implementations can legitimately diverge and no v0.3 requirement
would notice. Two of those divergences were **reproduced against the reference
implementation**, not hypothesised. A contract that omits them cannot deliver the readiness
criterion in v0.3 §9. That, and only that, is what v0.4 adds.

**What v0.4 explicitly does NOT do.** It does not resolve any AD-CA domain; it does not
promote any candidate; it does not incorporate ADR-002…ADR-006 (which **do not exist** — see
`01_ADR_REVIEW.md` §0); it does not change SPEC-002's readiness status, which remains
**NOT READY**.

## 2. §4.1a — Source Representation and Repository Identity *(NEW)*

Inserted after v0.3 §4.1. Motivated by two facts established in review, not by any preference
about the answer.

- **REQ-002-035**: The future specification MUST define, for each document in the Source Set,
  **which stored representation is authoritative** where more than one representation of the
  same document exists in the repository.

  > *Basis.* Every APS document in the repository carries a footer of the form
  > *"Source: Original text preserved in `<name>_260723_NNNNNN.txt`"*, and both a `.txt` and a
  > `.pdf` of each document exist at repository root alongside the `.md` rendering. These
  > representations are not byte-identical. A source boundary that names a *document* without
  > naming a *representation* does not determine a byte sequence, and therefore cannot
  > determine a hash. This requirement does not state which representation is authoritative.

- **REQ-002-041**: The future specification MUST identify the **authoritative repository**
  for the Source Set by immutable designation, and MUST record the status of any other
  repository bearing the same or a similar name (mirror, superseded, abandoned, or
  unrelated).

  > *Basis.* Two distinct repositories are currently named `aura-specification`
  > (`AuraIDToken/aura-specification`, populated; `aura-nomos/aura-specification`, an empty
  > stub whose CODEOWNERS mirrors the populated repository's layout). REQ-002-003 already
  > requires "repository location" as part of Source Document Identity; that requirement is
  > unsatisfiable while two locations claim the same name and no supersession is recorded.
  > This requirement does not designate either repository.

> **ARCHITECTURE NOTE — repository identity is UNRESOLVED (AD-CA-001).**
> Recorded as OD-001. Until resolved, AD-CA-001 cannot be closed even in principle.

## 3. §4.3a — Arithmetic Semantics Contract *(NEW)*

Inserted after v0.3 §4.3. **This is the substantive addition of v0.4.**

v0.3 REQ-002-014 requires one numeric representation covering "domain, width, sign, scale,
rounding behavior, overflow behavior, and byte order where applicable". Review established
that this is necessary but **not sufficient**: a specification can satisfy REQ-002-014 in full
and still admit two conformant implementations that produce different vectors, because
REQ-002-014 constrains the *representation of values* and says nothing about the *semantics
of the operations applied to them*. Two such divergences were reproduced against the current
reference implementation and are cited below as basis. Neither is resolved here.

- **REQ-002-036**: The future specification MUST define the **dimension (cardinality)** of the
  Constitution Vector and the **index ordering** of its components.

  > *Basis.* The reference implementation hard-codes 1536 (`core/embedding.py`;
  > `core/offline_normalizer.py CONSTITUTION_DIM = 1536`, enforced by `ValueError`), while
  > v0.3 §6 lists a bare token `32` among AD-CA-007 candidates. Under either reading of that
  > token, dimension is presently **not owned by any AD-CA row**: if `32` means dimension it
  > contradicts every implementation and document; if it means integer width, dimension is
  > simply absent from the decision set. Cardinality and index order jointly determine the
  > canonical byte length and byte positions, so neither may be left implicit.

- **REQ-002-037**: The future specification MUST define the **admissible input-length
  domain** of the transformation pipeline, and MUST define the behaviour for each of:
  empty input, minimum-length input, maximum-length input, input shorter than the vector
  dimension, and input longer than the vector dimension. Where the defined behaviour is
  rejection, that rejection MUST be explicit. **Silent truncation, silent padding, and
  silent cyclic repetition MUST NOT be permitted**, consistent with the governing principle
  of REQ-002-031.

  > *Basis.* `core/embedding.py` computes
  > `[base_pattern[i % len(base_pattern)] for i in range(1536)]`, which cyclically **tiles**
  > short inputs and silently **truncates** inputs longer than 1536 characters. The AURA
  > Constitution is on the order of 6,500 characters, so on the most plausible real input the
  > current transform discards the large majority of the source without signalling. Neither
  > behaviour is specified anywhere. Additionally the two modules disagree on the empty case:
  > `embed_text("")` returns a zero vector, while `offline_normalizer.normalize_vector`
  > raises `ValueError("Cannot normalize zero vector")` on exactly that input.

- **REQ-002-038**: The future specification MUST define **integer division and rounding
  semantics** in language-independent terms, covering at minimum the rounding direction for
  negative operands (floor toward −∞, truncate toward zero, or euclidean), and MUST state
  that this definition governs regardless of the host language's default operator behaviour.

  > *Basis — reproduced.* `core/evaluator.py` applies Python's `//` at two points
  > (`dot // SCALING_FACTOR`; `weight_semantic * sa // SCALING_FACTOR`). Python `//` floors
  > toward −∞; Rust `/`, C/C++ `/`, and JavaScript `Math.trunc` truncate toward zero. Executed
  > against the class with a negative constitution component (reachable via the public
  > constructor, which accepts any `List[int]`): `dot = -7000029999` yields **−70001** under
  > Python and **−70000** under truncation; on the ARI path `sa = -70001` yields **−49001**
  > versus **−49000**. A correct Rust implementation written against every document that
  > exists today produces a different ARI and a different drift. This defeats INV-002 and
  > INV-006, both Critical. *Scope note: the current `embed_text` emits only non-negative
  > components, so the demonstration path does not fire in the shipped demo, and the existing
  > x86_64-vs-ARM64 CI comparison runs Python on both sides and therefore cannot detect it.
  > The defect is latent and reachable, not a live production failure — but it is exactly the
  > class of accident that becomes permanent at freeze.*

- **REQ-002-039**: Where the construction pipeline includes any stage that converts
  floating-point values to fixed-point or integer values, the future specification MUST
  define **the rounding mode of that conversion explicitly** (including tie behaviour), MUST
  define the **boundary** at which the pipeline ceases to permit floating-point arithmetic,
  and MUST define whether any floating-point stage forms part of the reproducibility
  contract. Where a floating-point stage is within the reproducibility contract, the
  specification MUST additionally constrain summation order and MUST prohibit reassociation,
  fused-multiply-add contraction, and vectorized reduction that would alter the result.

  > *Basis — reproduced.* `core/offline_normalizer.py` uses
  > `round(x * SCALING_FACTOR)`. Python's built-in `round()` is **half-to-even** (banker's);
  > C `round()` and Rust `f64::round()` are **half-away-from-zero**; JavaScript `Math.round`
  > is **half-up**. Executed: `x*1e5 = 0.5` → Python **0**, C/Rust **1**, JS **1**;
  > `x*1e5 = 2.5` → Python **2**, C/Rust **3**, JS **3**. This is the Constitution **Vector
  > construction path**. v0.3 §6 lists `round-half-to-even` as a **candidate only** and §3.4
  > states no candidate constitutes a default — yet the implementation has already committed
  > to that candidate by using a language builtin. Separately,
  > `normalize_vector` computes `math.sqrt(sum(x * x for x in vector))`; IEEE-754 addition is
  > not associative, so this is stable only under fixed order without compiler reassociation
  > or FMA. The implementation's own `CONSTITUTIONAL_DECREE.md` Article VII *permits* float in
  > this one file, but permission is not specification.

- **REQ-002-040**: The future specification MUST require that any operation combining two or
  more vectors **verify dimensional agreement before computing**, and MUST define rejection
  as the response to disagreement. Implicit truncation to the shorter operand MUST NOT be
  permitted.

  > *Basis — reproduced.* `PoCAEvaluator.vector_similarity_int32` uses
  > `zip(v1, v2)`, which silently stops at the shorter sequence. Executed with a
  > 1536-dimension constitution and a length-1 agent vector, the evaluator returned
  > `{'ari': 0, 'drift': 200000}` — a confident numeric result, no exception, no diagnostic.
  > This violates INV-008 (Fail Closed, Critical) and Constitution Article IV P6, and is the
  > precise pattern REQ-002-031 prohibits.

- **REQ-002-042**: The future specification MUST NOT permit **tolerance-based or approximate
  validation** to establish, confirm, or substitute for canonical identity. Tolerance-based
  checks MAY be specified as diagnostics, in which case the specification MUST state
  explicitly that they carry no identity-establishing force.

  > *Basis.* `offline_normalizer.verify_unit_vector` accepts any magnitude within **±1%** of
  > 10⁵ (`tolerance = 0.01`). A ±1% band admits a very large set of mutually distinct vectors
  > as "valid". Nothing in the repository distinguishes a diagnostic tolerance check from an
  > identity check, and the function's name and position in the pipeline invite the
  > conflation.

> **ARCHITECTURE NOTE — arithmetic semantics are UNRESOLVED (AD-CA-007).**
> None of REQ-002-036 through REQ-002-042 selects a value. The rounding mode, division
> semantics, dimension, and input-length policy currently present in the reference
> implementation are **implementation accidents**, not approved decisions, and the
> reproductions above are cited as *evidence that the contract surface is incomplete* — never
> as evidence of what the contract should say. Recorded as OD-007, OD-011, OD-012.

## 4. §4.9a — Invalid Lifecycle Transitions *(NEW)*

Inserted after v0.3 §4.9.

- **REQ-002-044**: For each lifecycle state defined under REQ-002-028 and REQ-002-029, the
  future specification MUST enumerate the **invalid transitions** out of and into that state,
  and MUST define the response to an attempted invalid transition. The response MUST fail
  closed. The specification MUST additionally define **which authority** may effect each
  valid transition, and MUST state that lifecycle status is conferred only by that authority
  and **MUST NOT be self-asserted** by an artifact, document, or implementation.

  > *Basis.* v0.3 §4.9/§4.10 and §8 correctly separate registration from freeze, but no
  > document in the repository enumerates invalid transitions for any artifact class.
  > Separately, `VERSIONING.md` §3 reserves `APPROVED → FROZEN` to the Chief Architect under
  > Constitution Article XI, while the reference implementation declares *itself* frozen
  > (`CONSTITUTIONAL_DECREE.md` Article VIII "Frozen Iron Core"; `docs/architecture.md`
  > footer "Status: FROZEN — MC-READY 2026") despite
  > `reference/RI-PY_AURA_POC_A_CORE.md` recording `APS-950 Certification Status: NOT
  > CERTIFIED` and flagging the self-freeze as a governance problem. "Frozen" currently
  > denotes both a conferred governance state and a self-description, which makes
  > REQ-002-029's verification procedure unspecifiable.

> **ARCHITECTURE NOTE.** The lifecycle `DRAFT → VERIFIED → REGISTERED → APPROVED → FROZEN`
> referenced in review scoping **does not exist** in this repository. `VERSIONING.md` §3 and
> GOV-001 §4 both define `DRAFT → REVIEW → APPROVED → FROZEN (↘ DEPRECATED → ARCHIVED)`.
> There is no VERIFIED state and no REGISTERED state. Introducing them is a **new decision**
> affecting every artifact class, not a clarification. Recorded as OD-015.

## 5. §4.12 — Referenced-Protocol Completeness *(NEW)*

- **REQ-002-043**: A normative document MUST NOT reference, depend upon, or derive a
  requirement from a named protocol, method, or procedure that is not itself completely
  defined and approved. For any such referenced element, the following MUST all be defined
  before the reference is permitted: input domain; output domain; dimension; index ordering;
  byte ordering; arithmetic model; rounding; overflow; invalid-input behaviour; empty-input
  behaviour; maximum-input behaviour; determinism statement; domain separation; and published
  test vectors.

  > *Basis.* **ADP-001 ("Aura Deterministic Projection") does not exist.** No document,
  > identifier, or definition for it appears in the specification repository or in either
  > reference implementation, and the prefix `ADP` is not in the APS-000 §3 / Appendix A
  > canonical prefix registry. Assessed against the fourteen items above, ADP-001 is **0/14
  > defined** (`01_ADR_REVIEW.md` §7). Any ADR or specification that cites ADP-001 as an
  > available mechanism would be citing a name, not a protocol.

## 6. §6 — Unresolved Architectural Decisions — corrections and additions

**No decision domain is added, removed, renumbered, or resolved.** Two corrections and one
record:

**6.1 — AD-CA-005 scope restated verbatim.** Review input described AD-CA-005 as "vector
projection mechanism". The authoritative v0.3 §6 text is:

> AD-CA-005 — *Embedding method identity and versioning model.* Candidate:
> `Dictionary-Based Embedding` (candidate only). Blocks REQ-002-012, REQ-002-016, REQ-002-024.

These are different domains: method *identity and versioning* is a dependency-identification
decision; a *projection mechanism* is an algorithm-definition decision, which v0.3 places
under AD-CA-003 (pipeline) and AD-CA-007 (numeric contract). v0.4 **retains the v0.3 wording
unchanged** and records the discrepancy as **OD-002** for Custodian resolution. Adopting the
alternative wording without a decision would silently redefine AD-CA-005 and orphan its
actual subject matter.

**6.2 — Orphaned decision domains recorded.** A five-ADR grouping of the form
{ADR-002: 001,002 · ADR-003: 003,005 · ADR-004: 007,008 · ADR-005: 009,010 · ADR-006: 011,012}
covers **10 of 12** domains. **AD-CA-004** (normalization rules) and **AD-CA-006** (dictionary
identity and dependency closure) have **no owning ADR**. Both were verified present and
UNRESOLVED in v0.3 §6 and Appendix A(E); neither is inferred or filled in here.

This is load-bearing, not bookkeeping:

- AD-CA-004 blocks **REQ-002-021 and REQ-002-022** — the same requirements ADR-004 exists to
  close through AD-CA-008. **ADR-004 therefore cannot close its own stated scope while
  AD-CA-004 is unowned.**
- AD-CA-006 blocks **REQ-002-034** (dependency closure). No ADR in the grouping addresses it,
  so "same source → same vector" remains unprovable: an undeclared dependency may vary
  between two conformant implementations without either violating anything.

**Every AD-CA domain MUST be assigned to exactly one governance artifact before any ADR in
the set is accepted.** Recorded as OD-016 and BLOCKER-P0-004.

## 8. §7 — Traceability Matrix accuracy fix

v0.3 §7 marks absent Conformance Test and Evidence IDs as `FUTURE REF`, which is correct and
should be retained. v0.4 requires one additional distinction in the **Source** columns, which
v0.3 does not currently make:

| Marker | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *(resolves)* | the cited section exists and contains normative content bearing on the requirement |
| **DANGLING** | the cited section exists but is a TODO, a placeholder, or does not in fact address the requirement |
| `FUTURE REF` | identifier not yet assigned (unchanged from v0.3) |

Applying this to v0.3 §7: of 34 requirements, **5 have sources that resolve** (REQ-002-004,
005, 009, 026, and partially 031) and **17 are DANGLING**, the majority pointing at U-1
through U-6 above. The full re-marked matrix is in `02_TRACEABILITY_MATRIX.md` §2 and is not
duplicated here.

> This is an **accuracy** fix, not a weakening. A matrix that presents a TODO as a resolved
> source overstates readiness, and v0.3 §7's own closing rule — *"Test IDs and Evidence IDs
> MUST NOT be invented to make the matrix appear complete"* — applies with equal force to
> source citations.

v0.3 criteria 1–16 are retained unchanged. Added:

17. **Arithmetic semantics are fully resolved.** Division and rounding semantics, float→fixed
    conversion boundary and rounding mode, vector dimension, index ordering, input-length

    exactly one governance artifact, with no domain orphaned (§6.2).

    protocol, method, or procedure lacking a complete definition under REQ-002-043 —

    change to its owning document. In particular, APS-001 exists with normative content;
    APS-200 §8 defines a canonical serialization; and APS-200 §4 no longer forces

## 10. §11 — Readiness status

**SPEC-002 READINESS STATUS: NOT READY** *(unchanged from v0.3)*

v0.3's rationale is retained in full. v0.4 adds:

- All twelve AD-CA domains remain UNRESOLVED, and **two of them (AD-CA-004, AD-CA-006) are
  additionally unassigned to any governance artifact**.
- **ADR-002 … ADR-006 do not exist**, in any branch or draft form. No architectural decision
  has been incorporated into this or any version of SPEC-002.

  **no second independent implementation** of the Constitution Artifact surface against which
  to test it. `aura-poc-a-core-v3.3` contains a self-declared *placeholder* embedding and
  **no code path that reads `AURA_CONSTITUTION.md`** — its `generate_sample_constitution`
  builds a vector from the synthetic pattern `0.5 + 0.1 * (i % 10)`, unrelated to the
  Constitution text. `aura-guard-v1.3` contains **no** constitution, vector, or ARI code at
  all. Cross-language replay (E12) has no second party.
- Two concrete cross-language divergences were **reproduced** in the reference implementation
  (integer division rounding; float→fixed tie rounding). They demonstrate that the v0.3
  contract surface, as drafted, is not sufficient to force a single outcome — which is
  precisely the condition REQ-002-032 defines as NOT READY.
- CR-007 remains BLOCKED, and remains **undefined**: no document in either repository
  specifies its inputs, outputs, authority, or pass/fail semantics.
- No Constitution Artifact and no Constitution Vector was created, generated, registered, or
  frozen by this document.
- No implementation was modified by this document.
