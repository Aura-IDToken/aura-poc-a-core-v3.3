## Authority-status vocabulary used in this matrix

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **UNRESOLVED** | No decision exists in any source for this domain. |
| **NON-NORMATIVE CANDIDATE** | A concrete value/algorithm/model exists in repository material, without authority to establish it. |
| **DISPUTED AUTHORITY — SCOPE UNRESOLVED** | A source asserts binding force, but whether its scope reaches a normative cross-language ARI is itself undecided. **This is not a NORMATIVE entry.** |
| **NORMATIVE** | An existing source genuinely holds normative authority for this specific domain. |

> **No domain in this matrix is marked NORMATIVE.** Per RD-1 (CLOSED): no normative ARI
> definition exists. No NORMATIVE entry has been manufactured to fill a gap.

## Reading notes

1. **No NORMATIVE row.** Every domain resolves to UNRESOLVED. Two domains additionally carry a
   DISPUTED AUTHORITY — SCOPE UNRESOLVED marker (Domains 4 and 10, via
   `CONSTITUTIONAL_DECREE.md` Article I §8), which is explicitly **not** a NORMATIVE entry and
   is the subject of open questions OQ-A/OQ-B in `09_OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`.
2. **Contradictions are recorded, not resolved.** Domains 1 (acronym), 4 (Q16.16 vs decimal
   scale), 7 (ADR-005 text vs operator behaviour), 10 (drift docstring vs code), 12
   (documented clamp vs implemented clamp), 15 (three canonicalizations).
3. **Candidates are not ranked.** The candidate column lists what exists, in discovery order
   within each domain. Order carries no preference.
